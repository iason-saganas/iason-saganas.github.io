Iason Saganas Ιάσων Σαγάνας

Astrophysics PhD student in Munich, at LMU and the Max Planck Institute for Astrophysics.

In my PhD I work on the interstellar medium: I reconstruct three-dimensional maps of dust extinction properties and of magnetic fields traced by starlight polarization, using Bayesian methods (Information Field Theory). I am particularly interested in dust as a cosmological foreground, for example in the search for inflationary B-modes in the CMB, or for dust corrections in supernova cosmology. I am also broadly interested in gravitational-wave science.
